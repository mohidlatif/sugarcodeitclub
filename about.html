About us

Engineering should be something you do.

Sugar Code It gives Clements students a place to explore coding, electronics and engineering through hands-on learning, collaborative projects and community service.

Our mission

Make technical learning approachable, practical and collaborative. New members should be able to walk in with curiosity, learn a skill, contribute to a team and leave with something they actually built.

Open to all grade levels

Beginners are welcome. Experience helps, but curiosity and willingness to participate matter more.
