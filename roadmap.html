Road Map | 2025-2027

Grow the club, then make the growth sustainable.

The timeline starts in 2025, when the club's website and digital organization became part of its infrastructure, and follows the work toward a stronger, better documented 2026-27 program.

A road map for the organization

The goal is not just a better website. It is a repeatable system for learning, projects, outreach, documentation and future officer handoff.

Principle

Build once. Improve every year.

Each stage should leave behind something useful: clearer information, reusable lessons, documented projects, measurable impact or a better handoff for future officers.

2025 | Digital foundation

Establish the club's online presence

Website work begins under the Webmaster role, giving students a clearer way to discover meetings, activities and opportunities.

Website Information access Digital organization
Spring 2026 | Documentation

Make club activity visible

Capture meeting photos, student participation and hands-on activities so the club builds a record of what members actually do.

Move beyond a one-page website

Organize the club website into clear pages for meetings, learning resources, projects, impact and membership.

2026-27 | Member pathway

Create progression for members

Help beginners move from basic lessons to build challenges, project teams, teaching and leadership instead of attending disconnected meetings.

Beginner to project Mentorship Leadership
2026-27 | Measurable impact

Track outcomes without guessing

Record verified projects completed, workshops delivered, volunteer activity and students reached so the Impact page reflects real work.

Projects completed Outreach Verified metrics
2027 | Handoff

Leave future officers a working system

Organize resources, project documentation and website structure so the next leadership team can continue improving the club rather than rebuilding from scratch.
