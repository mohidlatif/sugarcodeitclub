What We Do

Learn. Build. Share.

Members develop technical skills, apply them through projects, and use what they learn in outreach and service activities.

Learn

Learn Arduino programming, electronics, circuits, sensors, and engineering fundamentals.

Build

Work with other members to prototype, test, debug, and improve real projects.

Share

Use what you learn beyond club meetings through teaching, demonstrations, workshops, and outreach.

Outreach

Taking STEM beyond the club

Sugar Code It creates opportunities for members to use their technical skills outside regular meetings through teaching, volunteering, demonstrations, and community events.

Members have connected with STEM service opportunities through organizations such as Steel City Codes and STEM Spark.

The club has also represented Sugar Code It at events such as Ranger Camp, introducing other Clements students to the club and its hands-on STEM activities.
